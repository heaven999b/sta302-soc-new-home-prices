# Group 62 Task Checklist

This checklist breaks the project into concrete tasks and deliverables so that members can confirm responsibilities in the group chat. It supplements the existing Part 1 schedule. The proposal and its current assignments remain unchanged.

For an unassigned task, volunteer in the group chat and agree on one lead, any supporting members, and a completion time. A shared task still needs someone to coordinate it. Post a link to the finished work or a brief progress update when materials change. The dates below follow the current schedule in Toronto time; undated review tasks must be completed before the relevant submission.

## First submission preparation

| Task | Deliverable and completion check | Timing | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complete individual details | Each member completes their own Part 0 name, UofT email and signing date, and records their actual proposal contribution in Part 1. | Before final PDF export | Each member |
| Check literature and citations | Check the summaries against the three cited papers, distinguish published findings from the project's interpretation, and verify that in-text citations match the bibliography. Record corrections or confirm the check. | Before final proposal review | Lead to be agreed |
| Review the proposal | Review the research question, variable definitions, ethics discussion, interpretation of results, section word limits and layout. Provide specific edits and resolve outstanding comments. | Before final PDF export | Lead to be agreed; all members review |
| Independently reproduce the analysis | Download a fresh repository copy and run Part1_Code.Rmd with the supplied data and fixed split. Compare the generated sample sizes, tables and figures with the proposal. Record the outcome and any errors. | Before first submission | Lead to be agreed; preferably someone other than the original analyst |
| Prepare and submit Part 0 and Part 1 | Export the completed agreement and proposal as PDFs; check the matching Rmd and UofT OneDrive access. One member uploads both PDFs and the Rmd to Quercus, adds the data link in a submission comment, uploads the agreement to the group page, and shares confirmation of submission. | November 5, 2026, 11:59 p.m. ET | One uploader to be agreed; group confirms final files |

The ordinary submission deadline above is separate from the later project milestones below. Use the course's separate NQA submission route only if the group chooses that extension.

## Analysis and presentation milestones

| Task | Deliverable and completion check | Target completion in 2026 | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transformations and model diagnostics | Compare appropriate transformations, document the selection rationale, and reassess assumptions using updated diagnostic plots. | November 12 | Assigned in the existing Part 1 schedule |
| Variable selection and influence checks | Compare candidate models, inspect influential observations, and document reasons for retaining or removing predictors. | November 19 | Assigned in the existing Part 1 schedule |
| Robustness and final model validation | Check whether the findings remain stable under the planned sensitivity analyses. Freeze the final model before evaluating the held-out test set, and document validation results. | November 22 | Assigned in the existing Part 1 schedule |
| Design the poster draft | Organize the research question, methods, principal findings and figures into a readable poster draft. | November 27 | Assigned in the existing Part 1 schedule |
| Prepare poster text and review the poster | Write concise interpretations and limitations. Check numerical consistency, labels, citations and readability, then resolve review comments. | November 30 | All members participate; one lead to be agreed |
| Prepare the presentation and coordinate recording | Allocate speaking sections, prepare the script, arrange rehearsal and recording, and check timing, audio and consistency with the poster. Confirm the detailed course requirements before production. | December 2 | All members participate; one coordinator to be agreed |
| Check and submit the final presentation materials | Verify the final deliverables against the course instructions, check file access and formats, submit through the required route, and share confirmation with the group. | December 3 | All members review; one uploader to be agreed |

## Progress and contribution records

- Share a progress update at least once a week, with additional meetings as needed near deadlines.
- Keep written work in the shared Google Docs and commit code, data, figures and analysis outputs to the repository.
- Announce new contributions and material updates in the group chat with a link and a short description.
- Flag blockers or expected delays early enough for the group to adjust the plan.
- Record completed work in the Contributions section. Planned or newly claimed work belongs in the task plan until it has actually been completed.
